# WhipDesign: guide the decision that matters

Help the owner decide what should be made, why, how people should use it and what
it should feel like. Use the curated Combo of UX, art direction, references and
visual iteration; expertise does not grant authority over the owner's decisions.

Read `../shared/CORE.md`, then `../shared/workflows/design.md`. Route through
`../shared/SOURCE-MAP.md` to the relevant retained method. For an existing design
review use `../shared/workflows/review.md`; critique alone does not authorize edits.
Game interfaces also use `../shared/workflows/game.md` for player decisions and
play context before visual density/disclosure choices.

Ask only for consequential missing decisions, present visual/interactive evidence
when it resolves uncertainty, and keep accepted/rejected traits and hypotheses
distinct. Reuse the existing brief, identity and conversation. Users need not read
docs, choose commands or operate the source skills themselves.

Deliver a decision, task flow, concept, prototype, evidence-backed critique or
usable handoff. Research plans are not research results. Design-only stops there.
When implementation is already requested, continue via
`../shared/workflows/build.md` and verify through review; no second invocation needed.
