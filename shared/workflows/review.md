# Check the artifact against its purpose

Read the requested scope and actual artifact, relevant before state, brief and
latest owner feedback. Review-only does not authorize fixes. Check the dimensions
that affect this task, with a bounded selected method:

| Question | Method and required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Does the experience serve the job and important states? | Intent evaluate/journey/include/fortify as relevant; actual flows, errors, focus/navigation and recovery. Label heuristics separately from participant observations. |
| Does a game support the next player decision? | [game.md](game.md), Game UI Design checklist, actual first/repeated session and decision-information access; browser scene/input ownership where applicable. Preserve simultaneous comparison and useful playfield visibility. |
| Does it preserve identity and express the chosen direction? | Garden critique-guide/redesign-protocol; rendered evidence versus actual references and protected contracts. Taste/ frontend-design may challenge a named creative weakness within their scope. |
| Are there detectable implementation/craft problems? | Relevant build/lint/runtime checks and optionally Impeccable detect/action. Read findings, reject justified false positives, and preserve approved choices over aesthetic default rules. |
| Is the chosen comp faithfully implemented? | The selected image-to-code or Impeccable fidelity protocol, actual reference/captures and relevant viewports, not a self-assigned similarity score. |
| Do users succeed or prefer it? | Actual participant/task or owner-choice evidence with the claim at that scope. Screenshots, database recommendations and AI scores cannot certify this. |

Assess the actual render against the accepted direction and subject. A named
creative weakness may use one compact Taste/frontend-design challenge; this is
not a compulsory additional reviewer after the selected Impeccable finish method.
Familiar recipes, craft and identity are separate judgments. A pattern detector
does not overrule purposeful conventions or owner preference. Passing runtime
checks cannot discharge the relevant creative or player-decision question.

For a structural UX finding, compare the changed sequence/grouping/disclosure and
access to decision-critical information. A wording/spacing change only resolves
that finding if its observed cause was wording/spacing. Do not infer improvement
from fewer elements/clicks when necessary comparison, agency or recovery is lost.

Before calling a rendered check complete, inspect representative target viewports
and important interactive states. Exercise the meaningful behavior, not only the
page load. If rendering/tools are unavailable, report the exact unchecked dimension.
Keep reference read, tool execution, artifact change and outcome as separate facts.

For a substantial build, seek independent review only when authorized by the host
and task. Provide minimal raw artifacts, captures and the brief without build-thread
optimism. Otherwise take a fresh in-thread pass and disclose that limitation. A
selected Impeccable finish method uses its retained reviewer/degraded protocol;
do not claim registered helpers merely because files ship in the source library.

Rank material findings by impact, choose one causal fix batch, then recheck the
affected artifacts/flows. Default to at most two correction rounds, stop when a
round resolves nothing, and put remaining consequential tradeoffs before the owner.
Do not restart all source pipelines or polish beyond the user's scope.

Finish with the artifact, observed checks and open material findings. Separate
relative improvement from absolute acceptance. Do not infer that Whip outperforms
another skill from a functioning installer, clean detector or one favorable render.
